The Challenge

Most meditation apps treat every brain the same.

Mainstream meditation platforms offer the same generic content to everyone—regardless of whether you're managing ADHD, processing trauma, or working through depression. They assume a calm baseline that many people simply don't have.

The result? Users cycle through sessions that weren't designed for their experience. Engagement drops. Progress stalls. And the people who could benefit most from a structured mindfulness practice are the first to leave.

The problem isn't meditation. It's the one-size-fits-all approach.
