Neurotype Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Jan 25 2026    Last Updated: Jan 25 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LaunchSpace LLC ("Neurotype," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Neurotype mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Quick summary (high level):

  • Neurotype is a meditation app focused on personalization, progress tracking, and subscriptions.
  • We do not offer journaling or community features.
  • We do not run third-party ads or sell your personal information.
  • We use service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics/performance, crash reporting, subscription management) to operate the Services.

1) Contact

LaunchSpace LLC
Support: contact@launchspace.org
Legal/Privacy: contact@launchspace.org

2) Information We Collect

We collect information in three main ways: (A) information you provide, (B) information collected automatically, and (C) subscription information from app stores.

A. Information You Provide

  • Account information: email address and login/authentication details (depending on sign-in method).
  • Preferences and personalization inputs: settings (e.g., session length preferences, reminders) and wellness goals you choose (e.g., stress relief, focus, sleep). Choosing modules related to mental wellbeing may reveal sensitive information about you.
  • Customer support information: details you share when you contact support (e.g., message content, troubleshooting info). Please avoid sending highly sensitive information in support requests.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage and progress data: sessions started/completed, time spent, streaks, preferences, and feature interactions.
  • Device and network data: device type/model, operating system, app version, language, time zone, IP address.
  • Identifiers: app instance identifiers and similar device/app identifiers used for service operations, security, analytics, and debugging.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance logs to improve stability and fix bugs.

C. Subscription and Purchase Information

If you buy a subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play, we receive information such as subscription status (active/inactive), plan type/entitlements, and transaction identifiers. We do not receive or store your full payment card details from Apple or Google.

D. What We Do Not Collect (Core Experience)

  • No journaling: we do not provide a diary/journal feature for free-form entries.
  • No community: we do not provide community posting, comments, or social messaging.
  • No ads: we do not serve third-party behavioral ads in the app.
  • No wearable/health integrations: we do not collect wearable/health platform data as part of the Services.
  • No email marketing: we do not send promotional marketing emails (we may send service/transactional emails when necessary).

3) How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the Services: create/manage accounts, deliver content, track progress, and manage subscriptions.
  • Personalize your experience: recommend sessions and modules based on your preferences, goals, and usage history.
  • Improve and maintain the Services: analyze feature usage, fix bugs, and enhance performance and reliability.
  • Security and fraud prevention: protect accounts and detect misuse.
  • Customer support and service communications: respond to you and send account-related or security notices (e.g., password reset, critical updates).

4) How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below.

A. Service Providers

We use third-party vendors ("service providers") to help run the Services. They may process information on our behalf for:

  • Cloud hosting and data storage
  • Analytics and product performance measurement
  • Crash reporting and debugging
  • Customer support tooling
  • Subscription and entitlement management

Service providers are permitted to use information only to provide services to us and must protect it under contractual obligations.

B. Legal, Safety, and Rights

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith it is necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect the rights and safety of users or the public, enforce our terms, or detect/prevent fraud and security incidents.

C. Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

D. With Your Direction

We may share information if you direct us to do so (for example, if you request a data export).

5) Targeted Advertising

Neurotype does not use your personal information to show third-party behavioral/targeted ads in the app.

6) Data Retention

We retain personal information as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify personal information unless we must keep certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Residual copies may persist in backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

7) Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as access, deletion, correction, and data portability. You may also have the right to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

How to exercise rights:
Email us at contact@launchspace.org with the subject line "Privacy Request."
If available in the app: Settings → Clear Data → Reset Account / Delete Account

We may verify your identity before fulfilling requests. We may deny or limit requests where permitted by law (for example, to protect security or comply with legal obligations).

8) International Users and Cross-Border Transfers

Neurotype is available internationally. We and our service providers may process and store information in the United States and other countries. These countries may have data protection laws different from those in your jurisdiction. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for transfers.

If you are in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, we generally rely on one or more of the following legal bases: performance of a contract (providing the Services), legitimate interests (securing and improving the Services), consent (where required), and/or legal obligations. You may have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10) Children's Privacy

The Services are not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact contact@launchspace.org.

11) Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date above. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice as required by law (e.g., in-app notice).

12) Appendix: Consumer Health / Sensitive Data Notice (Where Applicable)

Some jurisdictions treat certain mental health–related information as "consumer health data" or "sensitive data." Neurotype may process data that could imply mental wellbeing status (for example, the wellness modules you choose and use).

A. Categories We Collect

  • Wellness goals and module selections you choose (which may imply mental wellbeing status)
  • Session history and progress metrics linked to your account (e.g., completed sessions, streaks)

B. Sources

  • You (your selections and preferences)
  • Your use of the Services (session history and progress events)

C. Purposes

  • Provide and personalize the Services (recommend sessions, track progress)
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Services

D. Sharing

We do not sell consumer health/sensitive data and do not share it for targeted advertising. We may share it with service providers only as needed to operate the Services (e.g., hosting, analytics/performance, crash reporting, customer support), subject to contractual restrictions.

E. Your Rights

Where required by law, you may have rights to access, delete, or withdraw consent for future processing of consumer health/sensitive data. To exercise these rights, email contact@launchspace.org with the subject line "Consumer Health Data Request."

F. Geofencing

Neurotype does not use geofencing around in-person healthcare facilities to identify or track individuals, collect consumer health data, or send health-related notifications or advertisements.
