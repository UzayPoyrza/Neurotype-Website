Neurotype Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Jan 25 2026    Last Updated: Jan 25 2026

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy ("CHD Policy") describes how LaunchSpace LLC ("Neurotype," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares "Consumer Health Data" (or "sensitive health-related data") through the Services, and how you can exercise your rights.

Why this notice exists: In some jurisdictions, certain mental health–related information (including wellness module selections and usage) is treated as "consumer health data" or "sensitive data." Neurotype provides personalized meditation and progress tracking, which may involve such data.

This CHD Policy supplements the Neurotype Privacy Policy: Privacy Policy. If there is a conflict, this CHD Policy applies to Consumer Health Data where required by applicable law.

1) Contact

LaunchSpace LLC
Privacy Email: contact@neurotypeapp.com

2) Scope

This CHD Policy applies to Consumer Health Data we collect about you through the Services, especially where you are located in (or your data is collected in) a jurisdiction that regulates consumer health data or sensitive health-related data (for example, certain U.S. states).

3) What is "Consumer Health Data"?

"Consumer Health Data" generally means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status, or health-related interventions. In Neurotype, this may include information that could imply mental wellbeing status based on how you use wellness-related modules.

4) Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following categories:

  • Wellness goals and module selections you choose (for example, selecting modules related to anxiety, stress, burnout, focus, etc.).
  • Session history and progress data associated with those goals or modules (for example, sessions started/completed, time spent, streaks, and progress milestones).
  • Inferences for personalization derived from use of the Services (for example, preferred duration or time of day), to recommend appropriate Content.

What we do NOT collect (core experience):

  • No journaling / free-form diary entries
  • No community posts or social messaging
  • No wearable or health platform integrations
  • No third-party behavioral advertising in the app

5) Sources of Consumer Health Data

  • From you: your selections of goals, preferences, and modules.
  • From your use of the Services: session activity and progress tracking events generated when you play or complete sessions.

6) Purposes for Collecting and Using Consumer Health Data

We collect and use Consumer Health Data to:

  • Provide the Services you request (deliver Content, enable personalization, and track progress).
  • Personalize recommendations (suggest sessions and modules based on your goals and usage patterns).
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Services (debugging, reliability, and performance improvements).
  • Prevent fraud and misuse and protect the safety and security of the Services.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms where required.

7) When and How We Share Consumer Health Data

We do not sell Consumer Health Data.

We do not share Consumer Health Data for targeted advertising.

7.1 Sharing With Service Providers

We may share Consumer Health Data with service providers that process information on our behalf to operate the Services, such as providers for:

  • Cloud hosting and data storage
  • Analytics and performance measurement
  • Crash reporting and debugging
  • Customer support tooling
  • Subscription and entitlement management

Service providers are permitted to use data only to provide services to us and must protect it under contractual obligations.

7.2 Legal, Safety, and Rights

We may disclose Consumer Health Data if we believe in good faith it is necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud, or secure the Services.

7.3 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, Consumer Health Data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections and as permitted by law.

7.4 Affiliates

We do not share Consumer Health Data with corporate affiliates.

8) Consent (Where Required)

Some jurisdictions require affirmative consent before collecting or sharing Consumer Health Data. Where required, we obtain consent through in-app notices and controls.

You may withdraw consent for future collection or sharing where processing is based on consent by:

  • Emailing us at contact@neurotypeapp.com with subject "Withdraw Consent."

9) Your Consumer Health Data Rights (Where Applicable)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access / Confirm whether we collect or share Consumer Health Data about you and obtain a copy of it.
  • Delete Consumer Health Data.
  • Withdraw consent for future collection or sharing (where processing is based on consent).
  • Appeal a decision if we deny your request (where required by law).

How to Submit a Request

Email contact@neurotypeapp.com with subject "Consumer Health Data Request." Include the email associated with your account and specify the request (access, deletion, withdraw consent).

We may need to verify your identity before completing your request. We will respond as required by applicable law. If you want to appeal a decision, email us with subject "Appeal – Consumer Health Data."

10) Data Retention

We retain Consumer Health Data only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. If you request deletion, we will delete or de-identify Consumer Health Data unless we must retain certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Residual copies may remain in backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

11) Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect Consumer Health Data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

12) Geofencing

Neurotype does not use geofencing around in-person healthcare facilities to identify or track individuals, collect Consumer Health Data, or send health-related notifications or advertisements.

13) Changes to This CHD Policy

We may update this CHD Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date above and provide notice as required by law.
