Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [Date]

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy supplements our general Privacy Policy and specifically addresses how Neurotype collects, uses, and shares consumer health data as defined under applicable state privacy laws, including the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar legislation.

1. What is Consumer Health Data?

Consumer health data includes information that identifies or could reasonably be associated with your past, present, or future physical or mental health status. This includes:

  • Cognitive assessment results and responses
  • Mental health and wellness information
  • Information about your cognitive patterns and brain function
  • Health-related preferences and goals
  • Any other information that relates to your health status

2. Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of consumer health data:

  • Cognitive Assessment Data: Your responses to cognitive assessments, test results, and cognitive profile information
  • Mental Health Information: Information related to your mental wellness, cognitive patterns, and psychological characteristics
  • Health-Related Preferences: Your goals, preferences, and interests related to cognitive optimization and mental wellness
  • Usage Patterns: Information about how you interact with health-related features of our Service

3. Sources of Consumer Health Data

We collect consumer health data from the following sources:

  • Directly from you when you use our Service, complete assessments, or provide information in your profile
  • Automatically when you interact with health-related features of our Service
  • From third-party services you connect to our Service (with your explicit consent)

4. How We Use Consumer Health Data

We use consumer health data to:

  • Generate your cognitive profile and personalized insights
  • Provide recommendations for optimizing your cognitive performance and daily life
  • Improve and personalize your experience with our Service
  • Develop and enhance our cognitive assessment tools
  • Conduct research and analytics (in anonymized and aggregated form)
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Sharing of Consumer Health Data

We do not sell consumer health data. We may share consumer health data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Explicit Consent: We will only share your consumer health data with third parties when you have provided explicit, opt-in consent for such sharing
  • Service Providers: With trusted service providers who assist us in operating our Service, subject to strict confidentiality obligations
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to affected consumers
  • To Protect Rights: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or others

6. Your Rights Regarding Consumer Health Data

You have the following rights with respect to your consumer health data:

  • Right to Know: Request information about the consumer health data we collect, use, and share
  • Right to Access: Request access to your consumer health data
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your consumer health data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for the collection, use, or sharing of consumer health data
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate consumer health data
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercise your rights without discrimination

7. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your rights regarding consumer health data, please contact us at [Your Contact Email] or through the methods provided in our Service. We will respond to your request within the timeframes required by applicable law.

To verify your identity, we may require you to provide certain information or complete a verification process. This helps us ensure that we are providing your information only to you.

8. Security of Consumer Health Data

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your consumer health data, including encryption, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Retention of Consumer Health Data

We retain consumer health data only for as long as necessary to provide our Service, fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. When you request deletion, we will delete your consumer health data in accordance with applicable law, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our Service after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
